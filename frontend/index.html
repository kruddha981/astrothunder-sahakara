THREE SIMPLE STEPS

How Sahakara works

The fastest path from extra food in commercial kitchens to hungry recipients nearby.

Step 01

Post

Commercial kitchens enter portion count, food classification (veg/cooked/dry), and safe pickup deadline via web, telephone, or SMS.

Step 02

Match

The automated dispatch engine calculates recipient suitability, distance, and driver proximity to confirm a verified shelter within seconds.

Step 03

Deliver

A nearby volunteer or shelter vehicle collects the surplus in insulated containers. Handover is validated via a secure 4-digit OTP.
